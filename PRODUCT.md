# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Treasury staff at the National Bank of Bahrain, on the ALM desk, the FX and money-market desks, the
Portfolio Management Unit (the bond portfolio) and the head of treasury. They sit in a daylit office in Manama with two monitors, Excel and the bank's
core systems open beside this client, and they use it all day: the dealers glance at the blotter and
the breaks between calls; the ALM analyst reads ratios against the bank's limits before the ALCO pack
goes out; the PMU desk watches its carry over FTP, its rate risk after hedges and its repo funding;
the head of treasury wants one page that says whether anything needs them.

## Product Purpose

A web client over the treasury server, in the browser or in its own Windows window: a golden deal blotter reconciled against Kondor,
Bloomberg and Quantum, breaks and their resolution, the intraday liquidity ladder, the FX desk's
risk and P&L against the bank's limits, and the ALM workbench (LCR, NSFR, the liquidity maturity
ladder, early warnings and the contingency plan, repricing gap, NII and NIM, EVE and stress, variance
attribution, interest rate differentials, funding concentration, the ALCO pack) against the bank's
own parameters, the day's FTP curves ALM publishes for every desk, and the PMU desk's bond portfolio
(positions, accrual and income, forecast, risk and hedges, repo funding). Success is a screen a
treasury professional trusts at a glance: the number, the limit it is measured against, and what
changed, with nothing decorative in the way.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, institutional. Three words: exact, quiet, Bahraini. The NBB identity is a red and
orange mark; the client carries the red in its wordmark only and stays neutral elsewhere, because on
a risk screen red must keep meaning "breach".

## Anti-references

- Bloomberg-terminal cosplay: black screens, orange text, dense monospace everywhere.
- SaaS dashboard templates: identical card grids, big gradient numbers, icons on every tile.
- Consumer banking app styling: rounded pastel tiles, illustrations, marketing copy.
- Toolkit defaults: one font at one size, grey boxes, blue selection, modals for everything.

## Design Principles

- The number, its limit, its standing: every metric shows what it is measured against and whether it
  is inside, with colour reserved for standing and always paired with a word or a glyph.
- Dense but ordered: tables and figures may be tight; sections, titles and whitespace do the grouping,
  not boxes inside boxes.
- One vocabulary: the same chip, badge, table header and card everywhere, in both themes.
- Familiar before clever: standard tabs, standard forms, standard menus; the tool disappears into the
  task.
- Light is the primary theme (an office in daylight); dark is a designed variant for the dealing
  room, not an inversion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text and figures clear WCAG AA (4.5:1) on every surface in both themes; status is never colour alone
(dot or word beside it); the chart series are the validated colour-blind-safe order; keyboard focus
is visible; no motion beyond 100 ms state changes.
