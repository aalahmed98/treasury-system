## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment: a treasury professional fluent in Linear, Bloomberg and Excel would not pause at the vocabulary. One typeface, one chip, one badge, one table, warm neutrals with red reserved for breach; no side stripes, gradient text, glass, display fonts or decorative motion; the modal is gone. Two places still read as template: the lower half of the Overview is nine cards in three rows, where the Operations row and the bordered activity list are not earned (the balance-sheet row is, since Limits is a table and the rest are charts); and developer furniture ships to the desk (the warning triangle and origin on the sign-in card, the origin URL in the status bar).

Deterministic scan (web/src): 3 findings, all `overused-font` on the three @font-face blocks in tokens.css. Inter is a deliberate choice recorded in DESIGN.md and legitimate in the product register, so this is noted, not actioned.

Browser evidence (six pages, light theme, detector injected under Playwright): `tiny-text` on 16 elements, every one the `.t-small` class at 11 px muted (section metas, timestamps, hint rows, the stat-cell sub line); `flat-type-hierarchy` on four pages (11 to 20 px, ratio 1.8); `monotonous-spacing` on four pages (4 px gaps in most of the sample); `line-length` 185 characters on the Setup introduction. `gradient-text` on body on every page is a false positive of the injection method (the detector's own inline source matched its own regex; the built CSS has no background-clip or gradient). No page errors and no console noise on any page. Overlays were drawn in a headless browser only; no user-visible overlay exists.

Where the two agree: the 11 px small text and the flat scale back the review's "lean-in, not a glance" finding for the head of treasury. What the detector caught that the review missed: the 185-character paragraph on Setup. What the review caught that the detector cannot: the missing diff before a bank-wide save, the attention list led by chronic items, the absence of undo.

## Overall Impression

The product reads as one system and the keyboard model is unusually good for an internal bank tool. The work left is not visual: it is judgement at the high-stakes moments (saving the bank's limits, dismissing a break) and editing what the first screen says. The single biggest opportunity is the Overview's first thirty seconds: lead with decisions, not chronic system health, and give the head of treasury the four numbers they sign at a size they can read from a lean-back.

## What's Working

- The attention list as the first element with a calm empty state is exactly what PRODUCT.md asks for, and every row lands on the right place.
- The keyboard model: arrows, Enter, Esc, n, g-keys, Ctrl K, and a hint row under every table so it can be discovered without a manual.
- Status discipline: colour always travels with a word or a dot, dark is designed rather than inverted, tabular figures align in every column.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] A bank-wide parameter save has no diff and no ceremony.** Setup saves a new version on a note alone; its editor shows about eighty inputs with nothing marking which differ from the active version. Why it matters: this is the highest-stakes write in the product and every user's limits move the moment it lands. Fix: show the change list (already computed in alm/tool.tsx) above the button as "What changes in v4", disable Save when nothing differs, make the button a full-size primary with one sentence of consequence. Command: /impeccable harden.
- **[P1] Attention is led by chronic system items and repeated by the bell.** Three identical "feed stale 22d" rows precede the mismatch that needs a decision, and the bell on the same page shows the same list. Fix: collapse same-kind system items into one row, split "needs a decision" from "system health", and make the bell count only what is new since the user last opened it. Command: /impeccable distill.
- **[P2] The top bar is a settings strip.** Theme segment, four feed letters with ages, bell, search and user menu: fifteen targets. Fix: theme into the user menu, feeds into one health pill that opens the detail, leaving tabs, search, attention and user. Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] The ALM assumptions rail treats every input as equal and explains none.** Twenty-four inputs on LCR, thirty on Stress, in four flat groups, with labels like "commit liq corp". Fix: collapsible groups with the inputs that move this tool's headline first, a one-line definition on each group, and the bank's value shown as a ghost beside any edit. Commands: /impeccable clarify, then /impeccable onboard.
- **[P2] Small text and the flat scale make the Overview a lean-in.** 11 px muted text carries every meta line and hint; the scale runs 11 to 20 px; the head's one number, LCR, is a 24 px cell among sixteen. Fix: small to 12 px, metas in text_2 rather than muted, a 32 px hero row for the signed numbers on the Overview, and wrap the Setup paragraph at 70 characters. Command: /impeccable typeset.

## Persona Red Flags

**FX dealer, power user**: "/" opens the palette instead of focusing the blotter search, so a counterparty filter is three steps. No keys for Acknowledge or Dismiss, so the queue still needs the mouse. The detail pane is capped at 240 px, so the Sources table scrolls inside a scroll. The positions panel open by default costs 340 px of blotter on a 1080p screen. No multi-select: twenty orphans are twenty acknowledges.

**First-week ALM analyst**: NSFR, EVE, IRD and Attribution are never expanded; "bank v3" is unexplained; assumption labels are database keys made readable; Setup opens to a near-empty page until the draft button is found.

**Head of treasury**: the list answers "does anything need me" but not "what changed since this morning"; "7 ambiguous breaks, open the Breaks tab" is desk work surfaced to the head; 13 px body and 11 px metas on a 24-inch monitor; the number they sign off on is one cell among sixteen.

**Accessibility-dependent user**: the table sets role="grid" but rows carry no row or gridcell roles; the column resizer is a mouse-only div; abbreviation meanings and gauge values live only in title attributes; at 200% zoom the fifteen top-bar targets wrap into the page.

## Minor Observations

- Gap chart labels collide ("274m" and "155m"); Bars labels every bar, against the selective-label rule; NII by month carries twelve near-identical labels.
- Stress "Rate scenarios" has a Limit column that is empty in every row.
- "Deals on Wed 04 Nov · 3" is the weakest stat cell.
- Breaks table: Field, Expected and Observed are blank for 25 of 31 rows.
- "Refresh" appears in both the status bar and the ladder toolbar; the status bar shows the origin URL.
- Sign-in: the warning triangle beside the origin has no words.
- Casing: BOOKED, unmatched, ORPHAN, orphan across one row of the product.
- Spacing: 4 px gaps dominate the sampled rhythm on four pages.
