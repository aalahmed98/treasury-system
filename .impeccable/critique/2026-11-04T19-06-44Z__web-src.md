## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment: passes. One typeface, one chip, one badge, one table, one card; red only for breach and the wordmark; no side stripes, gradient text, glass or decorative motion; the only modal-like surface is the palette. The hero row is real data with its limit and standing, so it is not the hero-metric template. Two tells remain: the balance-sheet charts row is four equal chart-in-a-box cards and the Overview is nine cards deep below the fold; and the NII tool labels twelve near-identical bars while Gap labels only the extremes, so the labelling rule is per chart rather than per system.

Deterministic scan (web/src): 4 findings. Three `overused-font` on the font-face blocks (Inter is deliberate and recorded in DESIGN.md). One `side-tab` on the assumption group's disclosure chevron, a zero-size CSS triangle, not a card accent: a false positive.

Browser evidence (six pages plus the Setup draft, detector loaded from a local server so last time's inline artefact did not recur; zero page errors): `tiny-text` is gone from every page after the 12 px change. Remaining: `single-font` on every page (one family by design); a flat type scale of 11 to 20 px on four pages (ratio 1.8, with the detector's own banner adding 16 and 18 px in the second pass); `line-length` 91 characters on the Setup paragraph despite the 70ch cap, since the detector counts characters and `ch` measures a zero; in the draft editor, three eyebrow labels over 30 characters flagged as all-caps body, and one `low-contrast` on the disabled Save button, which WCAG exempts as inactive.

Where they agree: the flat scale and the long Setup paragraph. Detector caught, review missed: the three long eyebrow labels in the draft editor. Review caught, detector cannot: bulk actions, stale state during a recompute, the bell's grouping, the chart labelling rule.

## Overall Impression

The product now answers its two questions in the first screen: the head's numbers and the dealer's decisions. What remains is mostly the long tail of the dealer's day (twenty identical orphans worked one at a time), a moment of ambiguity during an ALM recompute, and a lower half of the Overview that is still a wall of cards. Nothing left is a showstopper.

## What's Working

- The decision list with system health beneath it, and the hero row with its standing in words, make the Overview answer both the dealer's and the head's question in the first 600 px.
- The ALM rail's group help, closed tails with counts and the bank ghost turn a wall of inputs into a readable instrument; the Setup save box is the clearest high-stakes control in the product.
- The keyboard model is complete end to end: jump, filter, select, open, act, close.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Bulk actions on breaks.** Nineteen orphans and seventy-four missing breaks are worked one at a time. Fix: Shift-click and Ctrl-click selection in the table, "Acknowledge N" and "Dismiss N with one note" in the actions pane, with the same confirm step. Command: /impeccable harden.
- **[P2] Stale state during an ALM recompute.** After an edit the headline keeps the old figure with only "computing…" in the title row. Fix: dim the results while the request runs, a skeleton line under the headline, and "Try again" in the error path. Command: /impeccable polish.
- **[P2] The Overview's lower half is still a card wall and the page is long.** Fix: one surface with hairline dividers for the four balance-sheet charts, Trading above Balance sheet for users who trade, two screens at most. Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P3] The bell and the page disagree on grouping.** Fix: reuse the page's decision-versus-system split in the bell, system health as one line at the end. Command: /impeccable distill.
- **[P3] Every-bar labels on NII by month and the stress charts; three long eyebrow labels in the draft editor.** Fix: `labels="extremes"` on those charts; shorten "NSFR thresholds and factors (< 6M · 6M-1Y · ≥ 1Y)" and its siblings to a label plus a help line. Command: /impeccable polish.

## Persona Red Flags

**FX dealer, power user**: no bulk acknowledge or dismiss; the Sources column still reads "KB··"; View and Export take toolbar space on every visit; the detail pane at 40vh shows four Sources rows on a 1080p screen before scrolling.

**First-week ALM analyst**: the hero row says "within limits · internal minimum 110%" without saying there is also a regulatory minimum; "bank v3" still unexplained in place; the ALM strip's Dashboard chip sits outside any group; the IRD tool has no assumptions rail and so no help line.

**Head of treasury**: the hero row is the win; still no "since yesterday" delta on LCR or NSFR, and the LCR trend's spike to 502% is unannotated; "74 missing breaks" leads the decision list for a head who cannot act on it.

**Accessibility-dependent user**: the collapsible groups are keyboard-operable; the gauge in the Limits table carries its values only in a tooltip; the hint rows under tables are not associated with the table; the disabled Save button's 2.3:1 contrast is exempt but low.
